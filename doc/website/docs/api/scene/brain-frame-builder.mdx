# BrainFrameBuilder

**Namespace:** `mne::scene` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Library:** Scene Library &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Source:** [`scene/brain_frame.h`](https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-cpp/blob/staging/src/libs/mne/scene/brain_frame.h)

`#include <mne/scene/brain_frame.h>`

```cpp
class mne::scene::BrainFrameBuilder
```

Describes the frames of the brain view as [`GpuFrame`](/docs/api/scene/gpu-frame) for any backend.

A frame is begun with `begin()`, filled pane by pane with surfaces, instanced glyphs and the screen overlay in drawing order, and taken with `finish()`. Meshes are identified by a key of the caller; their streams are copied into `resources()` when their generation changes, and dropped when a frame does not draw them for a while or on `release()`.

---

## Public Methods

### BrainFrameBuilder()

Creates a builder with the pipelines of every brain shader.

---

### begin(target, clear)

Starts a frame.

**Parameters:**

- **target** : *const [GpuFrameTarget](/docs/api/scene/gpu-frame-target) &*
  Size and pixel ratio of the target.

- **clear** : *[Color](/docs/api/scene/color)*
  Colour the frame clears to.

---

### draw_surface(key, streams, shader, uniforms, viewport)

Draws a surface; holographic and x-ray surfaces draw their back faces first.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the mesh, e.g. the address of its owner.

- **streams** : *const [BrainSurfaceStreams](/docs/api/scene/brain-surface-streams) &*
  Vertex and index streams.

- **shader** : *BrainShader*
  Surface shader: Standard, Holographic, Anatomical, XRay or ShowNormals.

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainSurfaceUniforms](/docs/api/scene/brain-surface-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/scene/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false for empty streams or a glyph shader.

---

### draw_instanced(key, streams, shader, uniforms, viewport)

Draws instanced glyphs.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the mesh.

- **streams** : *const [BrainInstancedStreams](/docs/api/scene/brain-instanced-streams) &*
  Shape and instance streams.

- **shader** : *BrainShader*
  Dipole (translucent) or Electrode (opaque).

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainInstanceUniforms](/docs/api/scene/brain-instance-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/scene/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false for empty streams or a surface shader.

---

### draw_overlay(overlay, scale)

Blends a screen-sized overlay over everything drawn so far, e.g.

labels and pane separators.

**Parameters:**

- **overlay** : *std::variant&lt; [Image](/docs/api/scene/image), [DisplayList](/docs/api/scene/display-list) &gt;*
  RGBA pixels (not premultiplied) of the target size, or a figure of the logical size.

- **scale** : *double*
  Texture pixels per figure pixel of a figure.

---

### finish()

Ends the frame and drops the buffers of meshes undrawn for kUnusedFrames frames.

**Returns:**

- *[GpuFrame](/docs/api/scene/gpu-frame)* — The frame, referring to `resources()`.

---

### release(key)

Drops the buffers of a mesh.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the mesh.

---

### mesh_count()

**Returns:**

- *int* — The number of meshes with buffers.

---

### resources()

**Returns:**

- *const [GpuResources](/docs/api/scene/gpu-resources) &* — The buffers, textures and pipelines of the frames.

---
