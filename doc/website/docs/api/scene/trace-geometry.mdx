# TraceGeometry

**Namespace:** `mne::scene` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Library:** Scene Library &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Source:** [`scene/trace_geometry.h`](https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-cpp/blob/staging/src/libs/mne/scene/trace_geometry.h)

`#include <mne/scene/trace_geometry.h>`

```cpp
class mne::scene::TraceGeometry
```

The vertices of all traces over a window around the view, and what to draw of them.

`stream()` fetches the vertices of every logical channel for the visible samples plus a prefetch of `prefetch_factor()` view widths on each side, decimated to the view's scale; while the view stays inside the window, less than a prefetch margin from its edges, scrolling needs no new vertices. `draws()` lists the traces of the current frame with their shader parameters.

## Public Methods

### set_prefetch_factor(factor)

Sets how many view widths are fetched on each side of the view.

**Parameters:**

- **factor** : *double*
  Prefetch factor, at least 0.1.

### stream(view, source, z_score)

Fetches the vertices of every logical channel over the window around `view`.

**Parameters:**

- **view** : *const [TraceView](/docs/api/scene/trace-view) &*
  The view.

- **source** : *const [TraceSource](/docs/api/scene/trace-source) &*
  The channels.

- **z_score** : *bool*
  Whether to replace the values by their z-scores.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether new vertices were fetched; nothing is fetched if no data lies in the window.

### draws(view, source, style)

Lists the traces of a frame; the uniform slot of a trace is its position in the list.

In butterfly mode every logical channel is drawn in the lane of its type, otherwise the visible rows top to bottom. Bad channels are red, hidden or missing ones are flat and of the background colour.

**Parameters:**

- **view** : *const [TraceView](/docs/api/scene/trace-view) &*
  The view.

- **source** : *const [TraceSource](/docs/api/scene/trace-source) &*
  The channels.

- **style** : *const [TraceStyle](/docs/api/scene/trace-style) &*
  Colours and scales.

**Returns:**

- *std::vector&lt; [TraceDraw](/docs/api/scene/trace-draw) &gt;* — The traces.
