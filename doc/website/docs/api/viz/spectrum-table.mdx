# SpectrumTable

**Namespace:** `mne::viz` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Library:** Visualization Library &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Source:** [`viz/models/spectrum_table.h`](https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-cpp/blob/staging/src/libs/mne/viz/models/spectrum_table.h)

`#include <mne/viz/spectrum_table.h>`

```cpp
class mne::viz::SpectrumTable
```

Spectra, channel selection and frequency axis of a spectrum viewer.

---

## Public Methods

### set_info(info)

Sets the measurement info and selects all its channels.

**Parameters:**

- **info** : *std::shared_ptr&lt; [FiffInfo](/docs/api/fiff/fiff-info) &gt;*
  Measurement info; its sampling frequency defines the frequency axis.

---

### info()

**Returns:**

- *const std::shared_ptr&lt; [FiffInfo](/docs/api/fiff/fiff-info) &gt; &* — The measurement info.

---

### set_scale(scale)

Sets the spacing of the frequency axis used from the next spectrum with a new length on.

**Parameters:**

- **scale** : *Scale*
  Spacing of the frequency axis.

---

### set_data(data)

Sets the latest spectra.

A new number of frequency bins rebuilds the frequency axis and resets the band.

**Parameters:**

- **data** : *const Eigen::MatrixXd &*
  Spectra, channels by frequency bins from 0 Hz to the Nyquist frequency.

---

### row_count()

**Returns:**

- *int* — Number of selected channels, i.e. rows.

---

### channel(row)

**Parameters:**

- **row** : *int*
  Row of the table.

**Returns:**

- *int* — The channel shown in `row`, or 0 when `row` is not selected.

---

### channel_name(row)

**Parameters:**

- **row** : *int*
  Row of the table.

**Returns:**

- *std::string* — Name of the channel shown in `row`, empty without info.

---

### spectrum(row)

**Parameters:**

- **row** : *int*
  Row of the table.

**Returns:**

- *std::optional&lt; Eigen::RowVectorXd &gt;* — The shown spectrum of the channel in `row` (the frozen one while frozen), or nothing before data for that channel arrived.

---

### select(channels)

Shows the given channels, skipping channels the info does not have.

**Parameters:**

- **channels** : *const std::vector&lt; int &gt; &*
  Channel indices in display order.

---

### reset_selection()

Shows all channels of the info.

---

### selection()

**Returns:**

- *const std::map&lt; int, int &gt; &* — Map from row to channel index.

---

### toggle_frozen()

Freezes or unfreezes the shown spectra; freezing keeps a copy of the current ones.

---

### frozen()

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether the shown spectra are frozen.

---

### set_bounds(lower, upper)

Cuts the frequency axis to a band and renormalises it to [0, 1] over the band.

**Parameters:**

- **lower** : *float*
  Lower band edge in Hz.

- **upper** : *float*
  Upper band edge in Hz.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — False when no spectrum arrived yet, so there is no axis to cut.

---

### freq_scale()

**Returns:**

- *const Eigen::RowVectorXd &* — Normalised frequency axis, 0 to 1 over all bins.

---

### freq_scale_bound()

**Returns:**

- *const Eigen::RowVectorXd &* — Frequency axis normalised to 0 to 1 over the band.

---

### lower_bin()

**Returns:**

- *int* — Bin of the lower band edge.

---

### upper_bin()

**Returns:**

- *int* — Bin of the upper band edge.

---

### bins()

**Returns:**

- *int* — Number of frequency bins of the latest spectra.

---

### data_rows()

**Returns:**

- *int* — Number of channels of the latest spectra.

---
