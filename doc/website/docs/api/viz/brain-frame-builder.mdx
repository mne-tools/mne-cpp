# BrainFrameBuilder

**Namespace:** `mne::viz` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Library:** Visualization Library &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Source:** [`viz/gpu/brain_frame.h`](https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-cpp/blob/staging/src/libs/mne/viz/gpu/brain_frame.h)

`#include <mne/viz/brain_frame.h>`

```cpp
class mne::viz::BrainFrameBuilder
```

Describes the frames of the brain view as [`GpuFrame`](/docs/api/viz/gpu-frame) for any backend.

A frame is begun with `begin()`, filled pane by pane with surfaces, instanced glyphs and the screen overlay in drawing order, and taken with `finish()`. Meshes are identified by a key of the caller; their streams are copied into `resources()` when their generation changes, and dropped when a frame does not draw them for a while or on `release()`.

---

## Public Methods

### BrainFrameBuilder()

Creates a builder with the pipelines of every brain shader.

---

### begin(target, clear)

Starts a frame.

**Parameters:**

- **target** : *const [GpuFrameTarget](/docs/api/viz/gpu-frame-target) &*
  Size and pixel ratio of the target.

- **clear** : *[Color](/docs/api/viz/color)*
  Colour the frame clears to.

---

### draw_surface(key, streams, shader, uniforms, viewport)

Draws a surface; holographic and x-ray surfaces draw their back faces first.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the mesh, e.g. the address of its owner.

- **streams** : *const [BrainSurfaceStreams](/docs/api/viz/brain-surface-streams) &*
  Vertex and index streams.

- **shader** : *BrainShader*
  [`Surface`](/docs/api/viz/surface) shader: Standard, Holographic, Anatomical, XRay or ShowNormals.

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainSurfaceUniforms](/docs/api/viz/brain-surface-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/viz/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false for empty streams or a glyph shader.

---

### draw_instanced(key, streams, shader, uniforms, viewport)

Draws instanced glyphs.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the mesh.

- **streams** : *const [BrainInstancedStreams](/docs/api/viz/brain-instanced-streams) &*
  Shape and instance streams.

- **shader** : *BrainShader*
  Dipole (translucent) or Electrode (opaque).

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainInstanceUniforms](/docs/api/viz/brain-instance-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/viz/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false for empty streams or a surface shader.

---

### draw_slice(key, image, quad, uniforms, viewport)

Draws an MRI slice: a textured quad whose dark voxels are discarded, blended behind opaque geometry.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the slice; also identifies its texture.

- **image** : *const [BrainTextureView](/docs/api/viz/brain-texture-view) &*
  Slice image; the shader reads the red channel as intensity.

- **quad** : *std::span&lt; const float, 20 &gt;*
  Four corners, each position (3 floats) and texture coordinates (2 floats), as a strip.

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainSliceUniforms](/docs/api/viz/brain-slice-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/viz/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false without an image.

---

### draw_video_billboard(key, textureKey, frame, quad, uniforms, viewport)

Draws a video frame as a round disk facing the camera, over everything drawn before.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the disk. Panes that each face their own camera need keys of their own, since a key holds one quad per frame.

- **textureKey** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the video texture, which the disks of all panes share.

- **frame** : *const [BrainTextureView](/docs/api/viz/brain-texture-view) &*
  Video frame.

- **quad** : *std::span&lt; const float, 20 &gt;*
  Four corners, each position (3 floats) and texture coordinates (2 floats), as a strip.

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainVideoBillboardUniforms](/docs/api/viz/brain-video-billboard-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/viz/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false without a frame.

---

### draw_video_decal(key, streams, textureKey, frame, depth, uniforms, viewport)

Projects a video frame onto a surface as a decal; with a depth frame the decal follows its relief.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the surface mesh, as for `draw_surface()`.

- **streams** : *const [BrainSurfaceStreams](/docs/api/viz/brain-surface-streams) &*
  Vertex and index streams of the surface.

- **textureKey** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the video textures.

- **frame** : *const [BrainTextureView](/docs/api/viz/brain-texture-view) &*
  Video frame.

- **depth** : *const [BrainTextureView](/docs/api/viz/brain-texture-view) &*
  Depth frame, or none for a flat decal.

- **uniforms** : *const [BrainVideoDecalUniforms](/docs/api/viz/brain-video-decal-uniforms) &*
  Shader parameters.

- **viewport** : *const [GpuRect](/docs/api/viz/gpu-rect) &*
  Pane in target pixels.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether anything was drawn: false without streams or frame.

---

### draw_overlay(overlay, scale)

Blends a screen-sized overlay over everything drawn so far, e.g.

labels and pane separators.

**Parameters:**

- **overlay** : *std::variant&lt; [Image](/docs/api/viz/image), [DisplayList](/docs/api/viz/display-list) &gt;*
  RGBA pixels (not premultiplied) of the target size, or a figure of the logical size.

- **scale** : *double*
  Texture pixels per figure pixel of a figure.

---

### finish()

Ends the frame and drops the buffers of meshes undrawn for kUnusedFrames frames.

**Returns:**

- *[GpuFrame](/docs/api/viz/gpu-frame)* — The frame, referring to `resources()`.

---

### release(key)

Drops the buffers of a mesh and the textures of that key.

**Parameters:**

- **key** : *std::uint64_t*
  Identity of the mesh or textured draw.

---

### mesh_count()

**Returns:**

- *int* — The number of meshes with buffers.

---

### resources()

**Returns:**

- *const [GpuResources](/docs/api/viz/gpu-resources) &* — The buffers, textures and pipelines of the frames.
