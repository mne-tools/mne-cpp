# DisplayCorrection

**Namespace:** `mne` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Library:** DSP Library &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Source:** [`dsp/display_correction.h`](https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-cpp/blob/staging/src/libs/mne/dsp/display_correction.h)

`#include <mne/dsp/display_correction.h>`

```cpp
class mne::DisplayCorrection
```

Builds and applies the SSP projector and the CTF compensator of a viewer.

---

## Public Methods

### reset(channels)

Resets both operators to the identity of `channels` channels and deactivates them.

**Parameters:**

- **channels** : *int*
  Number of channels.

---

### update_projection(info)

Rebuilds the SSP projector from the projectors and bad channels of `info`.

The projector is active when at least one projector of `info` is active.

**Parameters:**

- **info** : *const [FiffInfo](/docs/api/fiff/fiff-info) &*
  Measurement info with the projectors and the bad channels.

---

### update_compensator(info, to)

Rebuilds the compensator that takes the data from grade 0 to grade `to`.

Grade 0 deactivates it.

**Parameters:**

- **info** : *const [FiffInfo](/docs/api/fiff/fiff-info) &*
  Measurement info with the compensation data.

- **to** : *int*
  Target compensation grade.

---

### apply(data)

Applies the active operators to `data`.

An operator whose size does not match the rows of `data`, or that is inactive, is skipped.

**Parameters:**

- **data** : *const Eigen::MatrixXd &*
  Data, channels by samples.

**Returns:**

- *Eigen::MatrixXd* — The corrected data.

---

### projection_active()

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether at least one SSP projector is active.

---

### compensator_active()

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether a compensation grade other than 0 is selected.

---

### projector()

**Returns:**

- *const Eigen::MatrixXd &* — The dense SSP projector, with the columns of bad channels zeroed.

---

### compensator()

**Returns:**

- *const Eigen::MatrixXd &* — The dense compensator.

---

## Example

No example of its own: SSP and compensator of the viewers; applied by viz::qt::RtFiffRawViewModel and viz::qt::EvokedSetModel and exercised by test_dsp_rt_raw_display, it has no standalone use without a viewer.
