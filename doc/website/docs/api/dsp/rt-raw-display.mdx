# RtRawDisplay

**Namespace:** `mne` &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Library:** DSP Library &nbsp;·&nbsp; **Source:** [`dsp/rt/rt_raw_display.h`](https://github.com/mne-tools/mne-cpp/blob/staging/src/libs/mne/dsp/rt/rt_raw_display.h)

`#include <mne/dsp/rt_raw_display.h>`

```cpp
class mne::RtRawDisplay
```

Prepares blocks of streamed raw data for a real-time viewer.

---

## Public Methods

### set_info(info)

Sets the measurement info: resets the SSP projector and compensator, the filter overlap, the filtered channels and the SPHARA channel indices.

The info is shared, so later changes of its projectors and bad channels are picked up by the next `update_projection()`.

**Parameters:**

- **info** : *std::shared_ptr&lt; const [FiffInfo](/docs/api/fiff/fiff-info) &gt;*
  Measurement info of the stream.

---

### update_projection()

Rebuilds the SSP projector from the projectors and bad channels of the info.

---

### update_compensator(to)

Rebuilds the compensator for the compensation grade `to`; 0 deactivates it.

**Parameters:**

- **to** : *int*
  Target compensation grade.

---

### correction()

**Returns:**

- *const [DisplayCorrection](/docs/api/dsp/display-correction) &* — The SSP projector and compensator.

---

### load_sphara_bases(directory)

Loads the SPHARA basis functions of VectorView, BabyMEG and EEG from `directory`.

Missing files leave the respective basis empty, so SPHARA stays the identity for that system.

**Parameters:**

- **directory** : *const std::string &*
  Directory with the SPHARA basis files.

---

### update_sphara_options(system, first, second)

Rebuilds the SPHARA operator for `system` ("VectorView", "BabyMEG" or "EEG").

**Parameters:**

- **system** : *const std::string &*
  Name of the sensor system.

- **first** : *int*
  Number of basis functions of the first sensor group.

- **second** : *int*
  Number of basis functions of the second sensor group (VectorView magnetometers).

---

### set_sphara_active(active)

Turns SPHARA on or off.

**Parameters:**

- **active** : *bool*
  Whether SPHARA is applied.

---

### applies_sphara(rows)

**Parameters:**

- **rows** : *Eigen::Index*
  Number of channels of the data.

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether SPHARA is active and its operator matches `rows` channels.

---

### sphara_operator()

**Returns:**

- *const Eigen::SparseMatrix&lt; double &gt; &* — The SPHARA operator, channels by channels.

---

### set_filters(kernels)

Sets the FIR kernels.

The overlap is cleared and the start-up transient of the next block is dropped.

**Parameters:**

- **kernels** : *std::vector&lt; [FilterKernel](/docs/api/dsp/filter-kernel) &gt;*
  Filter kernels, applied one after the other.

---

### set_filter_active(active)

Turns filtering on or off.

**Parameters:**

- **active** : *bool*
  Whether the filters are applied.

---

### filtering()

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether kernels are set and filtering is on.

---

### filters()

**Returns:**

- *const std::vector&lt; [FilterKernel](/docs/api/dsp/filter-kernel) &gt; &* — The FIR kernels.

---

### max_filter_length()

**Returns:**

- *int* — The longest kernel order, at least 1 once kernels were set.

---

### filter_delay()

**Returns:**

- *int* — How many samples the filter output lags behind its input.

---

### set_filter_channel_type(type)

Selects the filtered channels: MEG, EEG, EOG, ECG and EMG channels whose name contains `type`, or all of them for "All".

**Parameters:**

- **type** : *const std::string &*
  Channel name part or "All".

---

### filter_channel_type()

**Returns:**

- *const std::string &* — The channel name part that selects the filtered channels.

---

### clear_filter_state()

Clears the filter overlap, e.g.

when the display is cleared.

---

### set_trigger_detection(active, channel, threshold)

Sets the trigger detection.

**Parameters:**

- **active** : *bool*
  Whether flanks are detected.

- **channel** : *int*
  Row of the trigger channel.

- **threshold** : *double*
  Detection threshold.

---

### trigger_detection_active()

**Returns:**

- *bool* — Whether trigger flanks are detected.

---

### trigger_channel()

**Returns:**

- *int* — Row of the trigger channel.

---

### trigger_threshold()

**Returns:**

- *double* — Trigger detection threshold.

---

### correct(block)

Applies SSP and compensator to `block` and detects trigger flanks on the uncorrected block.

**Parameters:**

- **block** : *const Eigen::MatrixXd &*
  Raw data, channels by samples.

**Returns:**

- *Corrected* — The corrected block and its trigger flanks.

---

### filter(corrected)

Filters the selected channels of a corrected block with overlap-add across blocks.

**Parameters:**

- **corrected** : *const Eigen::MatrixXd &*
  `Corrected` data, channels by samples.

**Returns:**

- *std::optional&lt; Filtered &gt;* — The filtered block, or nothing when the block is shorter than the longest kernel.

---
